## SECTION 1 — THE CORE FACTS (Headline Arguments)

### Fact 1 — Crude Oil Crashed 75%. You Barely Benefited.
- ✅ September 2012: Crude oil (Indian Basket) = **$112/barrel**
- ✅ January 2016: Crude oil (Indian Basket) = **$28/barrel**
- ✅ That is a **75% drop** in 3.5 years
- ✅ Petrol retail price in India dropped only **13%** in the same period
- ✅ Diesel retail price dropped only **5%** in the same period
- **Source:** PRS India — prsindia.org/theprsblog/explained-recent-rise-petroleum-prices

### Fact 2 — Government Raised Taxes 10 Times While Crude Was Falling
- ✅ Between **November 2014 and January 2016**, excise duty raised **10 times**
- ✅ Petrol excise raised by **₹12/litre** total in this period
- ✅ Diesel excise raised by **₹13.77/litre** total in this period
- ✅ Government's own words in Parliament: Done *"to take away gains arising from a fall in international oil prices"*
- **Source:** Business Standard — Lok Sabha written reply by Minister, August 2022

### Fact 3 — They Did It Again in COVID 2020
- ✅ Crude oil crashed **69%** in Jan-April 2020
- ✅ Government hiked excise on petrol by **₹13/litre** and diesel by **₹16/litre** in two instalments
- ✅ Petrol excise hit **all-time record of ₹32.98/litre** in February 2021
- **Source:** PRS India + Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman, Rajya Sabha, December 2021

### Fact 4 — Government Revenue Exploded
- ✅ 2014-15: Central govt earned **₹1.72 lakh crore** from petroleum sector (duties + taxes)
- ✅ 2021-22: That number rose to **₹4.92 lakh crore** — a **186% increase in 7 years**
- ✅ Revenue from excise grew at **46% per year** between 2013-14 and 2016-17 (when crude was falling)
- ✅ 2016-17: Centre earned **₹2.73 lakh crore** from petroleum taxes alone
- ✅ 2018-19: **₹2,10,282 crore** from petrol + diesel excise
- ✅ 2019-20: **₹2,19,750 crore** from petrol + diesel excise
- ✅ 2020-21: **₹3,71,908 crore** from petrol + diesel excise (88% jump in one year)
- **Sources:**
  - Business Standard — Lok Sabha reply by Minister Rameswar Teli, August 2022
  - Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman — Rajya Sabha written reply, December 2021
  - Deccan Herald — Lok Sabha reply by Minister Rameswar Teli, 2021
  - PRS India — prsindia.org

### Fact 5 — More Than Half Your Pump Bill Is Tax
- ✅ October 2021: **54% of Delhi petrol price** = central + state taxes combined
- ✅ Central excise alone = **31% of retail price**
- ✅ Delhi VAT alone = **30% of (base + excise + commission)**
- **Source:** PRS India — prsindia.org/theprsblog/petrol-and-diesel-prices

## SECTION 3 — PRICE BREAKDOWN (For the Donut Chart on Website)

### Delhi Petrol Price Breakdown (May 2024)
✅ Source: Razorpay/ClearTax citing PPAC data. Rajya Sabha Session 265, Question 776, 29 July 2024.

**Total Retail Price: ₹94.72/litre**

| Component | Amount (₹/litre) | % of Price | Who Gets It |
|-----------|-----------------|------------|-------------|
| Base Price (crude + refining + freight) | ~₹55.43 | ~58.5% | Oil companies (cost recovery) |
| Central Excise Duty | ₹19.90 | 21% | Central Government |
| Dealer Commission | ₹3.84 | 4% | Petrol pump owner |
| State VAT @ 19.4% (Delhi) | ₹15.39 | 16.2% | Delhi State Government |
| **Total** | **₹94.72** | **100%** | |

**Simple explanation for website:**
Out of every ₹100 you pay, about ₹37 goes directly to governments as tax.
The actual cost of making the fuel is only about ₹55.

### How VAT Makes It Worse
VAT is charged as a % of (Base Price + Excise + Dealer Commission).
This means when Central Excise goes UP, State VAT automatically collects MORE even at the same rate.
Both governments benefit when excise is raised.

## SECTION 5 — ETHANOL SECTION

### What the Government Claims (Verified)
- ✅ Ethanol blending grew from 38 crore litres (2013-14) to 904 crore litres (2024-25)
- ✅ Blending level: 1.14% (2014-15) → 20% (2025-26) — achieved ahead of schedule
- ✅ Programme generated ₹1.29 lakh crore in revenue for sugar mills
- ✅ Government claims ₹1,13,007 crore in forex savings up to Dec 2024
- **Source:** The Print, June 2026 — citing government data

### The Real Story (Verified / Cross-Verified)

**Who Really Benefits:**
- ✅ Sugar mill owners and politically connected distilleries are biggest winners
- ✅ Government sets and guarantees procurement prices — sugar mills get a captive market
- ✅ Current procurement prices (2024-25): Cane juice = ₹65.61/litre, B-heavy molasses = ₹60.73/litre, C-heavy = ₹57.97/litre
- **Source:** Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) official announcements

**The Water Crisis:**
- ✅ Sugarcane needs 1,500–3,000mm water/year
- ✅ Marathwada (Maharashtra) gets only 800mm rainfall/year
- ✅ Sugarcane in Marathwada expanded from 1.67 lakh ha (1970-71) to 10+ lakh ha (2011-12)
- ✅ Farmers pumping groundwater from borewells 1,000+ feet deep
- **Source:** Substack investigative journalism (KBS Sidhu, Oct 2025)

**Corn Farmers Are Hurt:**
- ✅ Grain-based ethanol (mainly maize) = 60% of total ethanol production now
- ✅ Domestic corn prices crashed to half of MSP
- ✅ Widespread farmer protests in corn-producing states
- **Source:** USDA India Biofuels Report, February 2026

**You Get Less Fuel for Same Price:**
- ⚠️ E20 fuel has lower energy content than pure petrol
- ⚠️ Mileage per litre drops with higher ethanol blending
- ⚠️ Price remains the same — so consumer pays more per km effectively
- **Source:** Multiple automotive engineering sources (need to add specific citation)

**Political Connections:**
- ⚠️ One firm linked to sons of Union Minister Nitin Gadkari saw revenue jump from ₹17 crore to ₹520 crore in 18 months
- ⚠️ Share price rose 1,700% — BSE put it under special surveillance
- **Source:** Dr. Arvind Dube, Medium, October 2025 (investigative, not official — tag clearly on site)
